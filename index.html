About Me

"Every Person Has a Story Worth Listening To."

"बोललात तर मार्ग नक्की सापडेल."
If you speak, a path will definitely be found.

Over the past 5+ years, I have had the opportunity to work with individuals across different stages of life — children, adolescents, students, parents, families and working professionals.

My work has taken me through counselling settings, schools, hospitals, non-profit organisations, community initiatives, child and mental-health care, elder care and industrial counselling. These experiences have taught me that every person is different, and meaningful psychological support cannot follow a one-size-fits-all approach.

My aim is to create a safe, confidential and non-judgmental space where people can speak openly, understand themselves better and work towards meaningful change at their own pace.

My Approach

Counselling That Begins With Understanding.

I believe effective psychological support begins with understanding the person, not just the problem. Every individual brings a different background, personality, experience and way of coping.

My approach focuses on creating a safe space, genuinely understanding each person's unique concern and working collaboratively towards practical and meaningful change — at a pace that feels right for them.

Therapeutic approaches used in practice:

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
Rational Emotive Behaviour Therapy (REBT)
Behaviour Therapy
Mindfulness-Based Techniques
Relaxation Techniques
Psychoeducation
Motivational Interviewing
The Process
01
Understand

The first session focuses on listening — understanding your concerns, background and what you hope counselling can offer you.

02
Explore

Together, we explore the patterns, thoughts, feelings and experiences that may be contributing to your current challenges.

03
Work Together

We work collaboratively towards goals that are meaningful to you, using evidence-informed therapeutic approaches tailored to your needs.

04
Build Coping Skills

Practical strategies and coping tools are introduced to help you navigate challenges more effectively in daily life.

05
Move Forward

The goal is lasting, meaningful change — greater self-understanding, emotional resilience and confidence to move forward independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions You Might Have.

It is completely natural to have questions before reaching out. Here are answers to some of the most common ones.

The first session is primarily a conversation — an opportunity for you to share what has brought you to counselling and for me to understand your background and concerns. There is no pressure to share everything at once. The pace is always in your hands. By the end of the first session, we can discuss how we might work together moving forward.
A standard counselling session is typically 50–60 minutes. The duration may vary slightly depending on the nature of the session and individual requirements.
The number of sessions varies from person to person and depends on individual concerns, goals and progress. Some people find a few sessions very helpful; others may benefit from a longer process. This is always discussed collaboratively during the counselling process — there are no fixed requirements.
Counselling is conducted with full respect for privacy and professional confidentiality. What you share remains confidential, subject to applicable professional and legal limits — for example, situations involving imminent risk to safety. These limits are discussed clearly at the beginning of the counselling relationship.
